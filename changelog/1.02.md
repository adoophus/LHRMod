### **Salt & Tears 1.0.2**

Up-to-date for PDX 1.2

## Gameplay

### **Military**

* **Forts now reduce local food production by \-20%.**
  * *Note: This makes the placement of forts more consequential. Capitals produce extra food to offset this penalty.*
* **Occupied capitals reduce the occupied nation's fort defence by \-25%.**
* **Occupied locations now lose control more severely.**
  * Changed from \-0.5 monthly control to \-1.5 monthly control.
  * *Note: First step at increased wartime penalties for being occupied.*
* **Wargoal ticking increased from 25 to 50.**
  * Ticking warscore will tick up 1 per month when the goal is reached instead of 2.
* **Mountains no longer block movement in winter.**
  * *Note: This removes an unfun block that made entire fronts freeze for months, making winter warfare a matter of attrition.*

### **Values**
* **Offensive** buffed:
  * **Assault Ability** increased from **\+10%** to **\+33%**.
  * **Logistics Distance** increased from **\+10%** to **\+33%**.
* **Aristocracy vs Plutocracy** rebalanced:
  * Aristocracy **Discipline** reduced from **\+10%** to **\+5%**.
  * Plutocracy gains **\+10% Burghers Pop Growth**.
* **Mysticism vs Jurisprudence** adjusted:
  * Mysticism conversion speed increased from **\+10%** to **\+20%**.
  * Jurisprudence research bonus reduced from **\+10%** to **\+5%**.
* **Traditionalist vs Innovative** reverted to vanilla.
* **Serfdom vs Free Subjects** reverted to vanilla.

### **Culture & Religion**

* **Assimilation speed increased by \+25%** to account for a reduction in vassal assimilation in the base game.
* **Culture acceptance and tolerance:** The minimum population requirements to accept or tolerate cultures have been removed.
  * *Note: Lowers the barrier for nations to interact with diverse cultures without being locked out by hardcoded thresholds.*

### **Miscellaneous**

* **Hourly ticks have been removed. All ticks are now day ticks. 4 speed is the default speed. Use 3 or 2 for war.**
* **Alliances now ignore relation requirements** as intended.
* **Complacency effects have been removed.** The disaster has also been disabled.

## Systems

### **Blockading**

* **Naval attrition removed from the first tier of coastal fort buildings.**
  * *Note: Blockades are not effective, fun or easy to maintain due to attrition micro. They should become an effective tool to hinder trade.*
* **Negative maluses applied to blockaded locations reduced.**
  * Removed max control and development maluses.
  * Food malus has been reduced to **\-33%** from **\-75%**.
* **Reduced maluses on a country that is totally blockaded.**
  * Monthly war exhaustion **\+0.4** has been changed to monthly legitimacy **\-0.2**.

### **Research**
*Research speed is one of the most important modifiers for economic scaling and reaching military power-spikes. Normalising it closer will help close the multiplayer power disparity.*

* **Research bonus given by liturgical language nerfed to +0.1 from** *+0.2*
  * *Note: 0.2 equated to +20% research speed at the base. Most of Europe across the games observed has sat at +3-5% while the entire arabic world at +20%.*
* **Base research speed has been increased from 1.0 to 1.1**

## Map

### **Population**
*The most accessible source of a nation's power, especially in Europe, is going to be it's avaliable population. These changes are aimed at unwidening the gap between major and minor powers. Our first focus will be on Central and Western Europe.*

* **France region pops reduced by \-10%.**
  * *Note: France was overperforming across the game due to raw population advantage.*
* **North Germany region pops increased by \+25%.** Luxembourg provinces excluded.
* **Brandenburg area pops increased by a further \+25%** (total **\+50%** on top of the region buff).
  * *Note: Brandenburg is being given a stronger foundation to compete with Bohemia and Poland.*
* **Scandinavia region pops increased by \+25%.**
* **Hesse area pops increased by +50%.**
* **Low countries population increased by +30% in certain places.**
  * The areas of Holland, Brabant and Friesland have had their populations increased by +30%.
* **Iberia Population Adjustments**
  * Castille has had it's population reduced by -10%.
  * Portugal has had its population increased by +10%.
  * Aragon has had its population increased by +10%.

### **Goods**
*The reduction of population in the world may have knock-on effects with food. To be safe and feed an enlargened populations in Central Europe we should buff the common food goods found there.*

* **Wild Game food value has been buffed to 4 from 3.5**
* **Fish food value has been buffed to 6 from 5**
* **Legumes food value has been buffed to 6 from 5**

### **Miscellaneous**

* **East Asia and Southeast Asia removed.**
  * *Note: These regions now have zero pops, no tags, and no colonisable locations. East Indies may be opened up late game.*

## Content

### **England**

* **Normandy** now starts as an English vassal. West Normandy locations are added to England directly, with forts adjusted to player control.
* **Hundred Years' War modifier** now additionally grants **\-33% Transport Ship Build Cost**.
* **England invasion prep modifier** buffed: Ship construction speed increased from **\+33%** to **\+66%**.
* England now receives a **\-33% Disembark Speed** penalty while at war with France during the Hundred Years' War.
* **English Cinque Ports** can now support building ships.
* **Age 1 Longbowmen Levy** unit buffed:
  * **\+10% Strength Damage Done**
  * **\+10% Morale Damage Done**

### **France**

* **France starts with 5 prestige** to reduce early cultural acceptance spam.
* **Brittany** starts independent.

### **Muscovy**

* **Prikazi government reform** now grants **\+1 Cabinet Seat**.
* Prikazi possible fire date moved from **1360** to **1345**, making it fire earlier.
* **Russian culture group integration advance** buffed from **\+10%** to **\+25%** integration speed.

### **Sweden**

* **Sala Silver Mine event** moved from **1500** to **1400**.

### **Poland**

* **Polish Hussar Legacy advance** added: grants **\+10% Heavy/Light Cavalry Power**.
  * *Note: The 1600 Polish-Cossack privilege that previously granted the same bonus has been removed to avoid a net gain.*

### **Ottomans**

* **Seljuk Roots advance** reworked:
  * **\+5% Discipline** replaced with **\-10% Army Maintenance Efficiency**.

### **Rome/Byzantines**
* **Heavy Cataphracts (Age 2-6)** nerfed:
  * **Strength Damage Taken -25%** nerfed to **\-10%**.
  * **Morale Damage Done +33%** nerfed to **\+20%**.

* **All Roads Lead to Rome** nerfed:
  * **\-5 proximity cost through roads** nerfed to  **\-2 proximity cost through roads**.

### **Order Commanderies**

* Order commanderies can now only be built on **towns or cities** (not rural).
