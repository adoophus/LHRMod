## *LHR EU5*

## **Salt & Tears 1.0.2**

**\-Gameplay-**

### **Military**

* **Forts now reduce local food production by \-20%.**
  * *Note: This makes fort locations and spamming them more consequential. Capitals produce extra food to offset this penalty.*
* **Occupied capitals reduce the occupied nations' fort defence by \-25%.**
* **Occupied locations now lose control more severely.**
* **Naval attrition removed from coastal forts.**
* **Wargoal ticking increased from 25 to 50.**
* **Age 1 Longbowmen Levy** unit buffed: **\+10% Strength Damage Done**, **\+10% Morale Damage Done**.

### **Values**

* **Aristocracy vs Plutocracy** rebalanced:
  * Aristocracy **Discipline** reduced from **\+10%** to **\+5%**.
  * Plutocracy gains **\+10% Burghers Pop Growth**.
* **Traditionalist vs Innovative** changes from 1.0 have been reverted. The societal value now uses its vanilla effects.
* **Serfdom vs Free Subjects** changes from 1.0 have been reverted.
* **Offensive vs Defensive:** Offensive buffed, defensive nerfed as per 1.0.
* **Complacency effects** have been removed.

### **Culture & Religion**

* **Assimilation speed increased by \+25%** to account for a reduction in vassal assimilation in the base game.
* **Culture acceptance and tolerance:** The minimum population requirements to accept or tolerate cultures have been removed.
  * *Note: Lowers the barrier for nations to interact with diverse cultures without being locked out by hardcoded thresholds.*
* **Jurisprudence** societal value research bonus reduced from **\+10%** to **\+5%** research speed.
* **Mysticism** conversion speed increased from **\+10%** to **\+20%**.

### **Integration**

* **Age 5 integration speed buff** added to the player integration speed auto-modifier.

---

**\-Map-**

* **Mountains no longer block movement in winter.**
  * *Note: This removes a harsh block that made entire fronts freeze for months, making winter warfare a matter of attrition rather than a hard wall.*
* **Topography updated to PDX 1.2 format.**

### **Population**

* **France region pops reduced by \-10%.**
  * *Note: France was overperforming across the game due to raw population advantage.*
* **North Germany region pops increased by \+25%.** Luxembourg provinces excluded.
* **Brandenburg area pops increased by a further \+25%** (total **\+50%** on top of the region buff).
  * *Note: Brandenburg is being given a stronger foundation to compete with Bohemia and Poland.*
* **Scandinavia region pops increased by \+25%.**

---

**\-Content & Setup-**

### **England**

* **Normandy** now starts as an English vassal. West Normandy locations are added to England directly, with adjusted forts to prevent AI Normandy from self-destructing.
* **Hundred Years' War modifier** now additionally grants **\-33% Transport Ship Build Cost**.
* **England invasion prep modifier** buffed: Ship construction speed increased from **\+33%** to **\+66%**.
* England now receives a **\33% Disembark Speed** buff while at war with France during the Hundred Years' War.
* **English Cinque Ports** can now support building ships.

### **France**
* **France starts with 5 prestige** to reduce early cultural acceptance spam.
* **Brittany** starts independent.

### **Muscovy**

* **Prikazi government reform** now grants **\+1 Cabinet Seat** (up from none).
* Prikazi possible fire date moved from **1360 to 1345**, making it fire earlier.
* **Russian culture group integration advance** buffed from **\+10%** to **\+25%** integration speed.

### **Sweden**
* **Sala Silver Mine event** (Sweden) moved from 1500 to **1400**.

### **Poland**

* **Polish Hussar Legacy advance** added: grants **\+10% Heavy/Light Cavalry Power**.
  * The 1600 Cossack privilege that previously granted the same bonus has been removed to avoid stacking.

### **Ottomans**

* **Seljuk Roots advance** reworked: **\+5% Discipline** replaced with **\-10% Army Maintenance Efficiency**.

### **Order Commanderies**

* Order commanderies can now only be built on **towns or cities** (not rural).

---

**\-Removals-**

* **Gold and Silver price changes** from 1.0 removed. PDX 1.2 made equivalent changes natively.
* **Iranian Gateway buildings** scrapped. Terrain buffs to Persian locations serve the same purpose more cleanly.
* **East Asia and Southeast Asia** removed from setup for performance.
  * *Note: These regions have zero pops, no tags, and no colonisable locations. Continuing the approach started in 1.0.*
* **Adjusted national advances** (Austria, Hungary, etc.) temporarily removed pending a broader advance rebalancing pass.
