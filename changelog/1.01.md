## *LHR EU5*

## **Salt & Tears 1.0.1**

EU5 1.10

**\-Gameplay-**

### **Military**

* **Mercenary adjustments:** Mercenaries have had their cost profile changed to incur a larger up-front investment.  
  * Increased **Mercenary Base cost** by **\+75%**  
  * Reduced **Mercenary Maintenance cost** from **150%** to **75%**.  
    * *NOTE: Mercenaries do not have a long shelf life. By throwing mercenaries immediately into battle, the mercenary maintenance cost  (which is scaled by size) is essentially subverted. For detailed math on the change, look at the bottom of the notes.*  
* **Regular hiring costs:** Regular hiring costs have been made cheaper.  
  * Infantry and Auxiliary hiring costs have been reduced by 33%.  
  * Cavalry and Artillery hiring costs have been reduced by 10%.  
* **Artillery siege bonus adjustments**  
  * Artillery siege bonus will now increase by \+1 per **2** artillery regiments instead of **3**.

### **Systems**

* **Bankruptcy adjustments:** Bankruptcies are difficult to punish because they do not make a nation geographically vulnerable for others to exploit. Making it more riskier and easier to punish.  
  * Added \+250% **Mercenary Base Cost**.  
  * Added \+250% **Mercenary Maintenance Cost.**  
  * Added \-90% **Fort Defensiveness**.
