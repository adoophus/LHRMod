## Gameplay

### **Military**
* **Days per siege phase reverted to 30 from 16**.
  * *Note: This change was unintentional, sneaking in from the tick speed changes*
* **Fixed unit construction requiring less manpower than the unit strength**.
  * *Note: PDX 1.2 changed unit build cost modifiers to also include manpower for some reason.*

### **Locations**
* **Integrated land no longer applys a -10% reduction to assimilation.**
* **Occupied land now reduces control faster from -1.5 to -2.5**

### **Diplomacy**
* **Send gift cooldown has been reduced to 1 year from 10 years.**

## Systems

### **Supply**
*Initial changes previously felt good but having good supply felt like an impossible task micro-wise with the loss of hourly ticks. These changes will increase from 3 months supply to 6 months to make engaging with the supply system feel more rewarding.*

* **Supply Depot capacity increased 2x from 500 to 1000.**
  * *Note: This stores 6 months of food for 35 frontage.*

* **Supply Depot capacity advances give an extra 1000 capacity per age.**
  * *Note: 6 months of food for 35 frontage roughly increases by the same amount each age, so this change makes it so it keeps pace.*
