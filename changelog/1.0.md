## *LHR EU5*

## **Salt & Tears 1.0**

Beta 1.1.6 (7c9d)

**\-Gameplay-**

### **Trade Goods**

*Note: Silver and gold front-load all their income value straight into the tax-base of a location. Its value should come more from trade. Trade ramps up more in the mid-game, therefore reducing some of the early-game benefits for nations over-reliant on these goods. Ownership of gold/silver now rewards nations with very high crown-power more.*

* **Silver and Gold** have both had their prices adjusted by **approximately \-20%.**  
  * Gold is now worth a base **market price of 8.0**. Its transport cost has been halved to **1** from **2\.**  
  * Silver is now worth a base **market price of 6.5** Its transport cost has been halved to **1** from **2**.

### **Military**

* **Tick speed changes: 2 ticks a day, down from 8 ticks.** Values adjusted, balance attempts to make combat last a bit longer, akin to halfway between EU4 and EU5.  
  * *Note: This is experimental. May be removed after the first session. Provides performance benefits.*  
* **Mercenaries nerfed:** Mercenaries now cost an extra \+150% ducats in **maintenance** per month.  
  * *Note:* *Paradox nerfed mercenary hiring cost too much, made them too accessible and increased regular training costs. On top of the manpower requirement and lack of required manpower for mercenaries while incurring cheaper up-front costs and equal maintenance it made regulars fundamentally obsolete.*  
    * *Testing math:*   
      * *Regulars 16.5k men \- **6600g** recruitment cost, **60g/month,** 16.5k manpower. Reinforcement costs. Building costs. Goods demand benefits.*  
      * *Mercenaries 16.5k men \- **1060g** recruitment cost, **60g/month**, no manpower, 10 yr contract. No reinforcement costs.*  
    * ***Under the new changes** mercenaries are costing around **150g/month** in this scenario, making them less of a crutch and necessity and more of a calculated long term saving/full int option.*  
* **Unit Research Merging:** Military unit advances from **Ages 2 through 4** have been merged by unit type.  
  * *Note: This reduces the total research burden, allowing experimentation with different army compositions without falling behind technologically. It also provides a much-needed safety net for nations with slow institution spread.*  
* **Starting quality value buff:** Increased the starting quality value for **Kyiv, Muscovy** and **Sweden** to 15\.  
  * *Note:* *This change allows these nations to be more in line with the rest of Europe unlocking Professional Armies, making them significantly more competitive early-game.*  
* **Experience decay reduced by 33%:** Drilled armies can maintain and reach higher values, making drilling more worth it.

### **Values**

* Traditionalist vs Innovative: The trade-off is now culture vs technology, with traditionalism offering better internal cohesion.  
  * Traditionalist **Estate Satisfaction Equilibrium** increased from **2.5%** to **5%**  
  * Traditionalist **Embrace Institution Cost** reduced from **\+200%** to **\+50%**  
  * **Culture Influence** (+100%) moved to the Traditionalist side.  
  * Innovative gains monthly literacy \+0.05%.  
* Offensive vs Defensive: Buffed offensive and nerfed defensive to make it a viable trade-off based on your nation’s playstyle.  
  * Offensive **Siege Ability**, **Assault Ability** increased from **\+10%** to **\+25%**  
  * Offensive now gains **Army Logistics Distance \+10%**  
  * Offensive now has **Fort Maintenance \+20%**  
  * Defensive now has **Army Logistics Distance \-10%**  
  * Defensive **combat speed** modifiers have been removed.  
* Serfdom vs Free Subjects: The modifier requires more use cases to make the trade-off of increased levy vs extremely strong national prosperity more of an impactful decision.  
  * **Serfdom Peasant Levy Size** increased from **\+20%** to \+**33%**.  
    * *Note:* *This might look large on paper, but in vanilla someone with 100 Free Subjects would be at a \-50% Levy Size, since Free Subjects has \-25% Levy Size, which has been removed in this mod.*  
  * **Free Subjects** now once again has **Prosperity Growth \+0.25%**  
  * **Estate Satisfaction** pressure reworked on both sides to carry clearer political cost. **\-10% Peasants Satisfaction** for Serfdom and \-**10% Nobles Satisfaction** for Free Subjects.

### **Diplomacy**

* **Knowledge Sharing:** Transfer institutions between nations that are within market range for 10% of the sharer’s monthly balance.  
  * *Note: This is a must for enabling playing outside of Europe in the future and the effectiveness of enabling certain tags to keep up in tech will be noted for any needed rebalancing around this change.*  
* **New War Goal: Destroy Foreign Buildings:** Declare war to remove all foreign-owned buildings in your country.  
  * *Note: Blocking new countries doesn’t remove already-existing buildings owned by other countries.*  
* **Base max diplomats** doubled from **1 to 4\. Increase unchanged.**  
* **Assassinate Ruler:** Removed from the game.  
* **Break Personal Union**: If you are a senior in a PU, you may choose to abandon it.  
* **Request Alliance**: Alliances between players no longer require positive relations.  
* **Remove Rival:** Cooldown reduced to 6 months.

### **Culture**

* **Integration speed increased:** Within certain thresholds of population (including your vassals), you will receive an integration speed buff to make minor nations more competitive:  
  * *Buff \+33% integration speed: Age 1/2/3/4: 750k/1.5m/2.5m/4m*  
  * *Ottomans tag excluded from integration buffs.*  
* **Assimilation speed increase:** Speed of assimilating cultures has been increased by 10%.	  
  * *Note:* *This change is mostly about reversing a nerf to assimilating cultures that was applied post 1.1.*

### **Religion**

* **Change main school:** Stability cost to change your main school has been reduced from 50 to 25 stability to be more in line with government reforms.  
* **Convert Area Cabinet Action**: Converts an entire area to the desired religion. Unlocked by a privilege given to the Clergy, at the cost of giving them extreme power in return *(+100%). This is slower at conversion province-wide than the normal action, but faster at area-wide conversion.*  
* **Religious conversion**: Conversion speed has been increased by \+25%.  
  * *Note: Experimental change, this may be subject-to-change midgame, but it is intended to give more reason for people to switch religion without harsh 100 year+ maluses due to the current time it takes to convert.*

### **Miscellaneous**

* **Destroy Market:** Removed the prestige cost.

## **\-Map-**

* **Tags reduced for performance:** China consolidated. Pacific Islands consolidated. Certain locations in these regions are not colonisable, have zero pop and have no tags. Removed society of pops. 15-20% performance impact averaged across 3 test games each run of vanilla and mod.  
* **New Topography: Valley:** Added to 23 locations across **Persia, the Alps, Serbia, Morocco, and Georgia**.  
  * *Note: Unlike Mountains, Valleys remain fightable during winter months. Their \+25% proximity impact makes them more accessible than Mountains (+50%). They start at \+25% so they do not disadvantage nations that get mountain buffs (paradox hard-coded impact cost modifiers)*  
* **Persian Gateways:** Three specific locations in Persia now feature unique mountain traits that **completely negate standard mountain penalties**, acting as strategic gateways in and out of Persia.  
* **Persian Harbours:** Along the southern Persian coast, a few locations have had their natural harbor capacity increased.  
* **New Straits:**  
* **Iberia to North Africa:** Crossing added between **Gabal Tariq** and **Sabtah**.  
* **Corsica to Sardinia:** Crossing added between **Bonifacio** and **Terranova**.

**\-Content & Setup-**

### **Low Countries**

* **Countries:** 5 countries have been released from France.  
* **Truces:** All countries in the low countries now have 15-year truces with France to reduce the effectiveness of crib-killing.

### **Hesse**

* **Locations**: Starts with 2 new locations, including the Korbach Gold mine.  
* **Advances:** 17 new unique advances added to the Hessian tree.  
* **Events:** 24 new scripted events covering Hessian internal politics and regional ambitions.  
* **Starting Disaster:** **Hesse Decentralisation** \- navigate appeasing protected vassals that cannot be annexed, choosing between staying in the disaster for longer and exploiting them for powerful gain or exiting the disaster to safely avoid unforeseen circumstances.

### **Scandinavia**

* Sweden now starts with the **Sodra Osterbotten** state in **Finland** fully cored and colonised in 1337\.  
* Denmark, Norway, and Sweden now start with a unique, non-buildable building in their capitals.  
  * **Effect:** Provides **\+8 Monthly Sailors** to bolster early-game maritime presence.

### **Bohemia**

*Note: While this may look like a big nerf on paper, it does not change the economical potential or the income of the nation too much. It is intended to keep them focused on their own regional consolidation early rather than blob the HRE.*

* Removed all RGO production buffs from Bohemian gold mines.  
* Moravia no longer starts as a vassal under Bohemia and is now instead a personal union.  
* Rothenberg was released as a vassal in the north on the border of Lusatia.  
* Most of Silesia has been released under 3 new silesian tags. A few Bohemian vassals remain. Two of the northern Silesians are guaranteed by Bohemia at the start of the game.

### **Brandenburg**

*Note: Extremely underperforming nation in multiplayer, completely unviable. It is bad for the health of Northern Europe and boring for Scandinavia to have no competitor to its south. This is a first step at making it playable. They are now a potential king-maker to Bohemian or Polish dominance of Eastern Europe.*

* Reconfigured RGOs in the vicinity or inside of Brandenburg to include lumber, fur and salt, with the primary focus of reducing the legume spam.  
* Moved robber barons to 1370 from 1350 so it comes sooner after finishing the initial stage of the disaster.  
* Initial disaster made both easier and faster to solve, with the initial control reduction changed from \-40% to \-10%.  
* The disaster will no longer give permanent control maluses while it lasts.  
* Increased population by 25-40% in certain locations.  
* Reworked culture amongst its locations, with the slavic culture pushed more towards the right to help with early game levies and control.

### **Switzerland**

* Removed Austrian starting locations within Switzerland.  
* Released various Swiss minors to create a more dynamic and contested Alpine region.  
* **Schwyz** now serves as the primary focus tag for regional consolidation.

### **Middle East/Persia**

*Note: These changes are designed to rebalance the Mamluks’ very early game pressure and pass the responsibility of early-game harassment of the Ottomans to European powers. The Mamluks are still very much a Tier 1 nation early on.*

* Ottomans now start with a 10-year truce with the Mamluks.  
* Persian tags have been made independent and larger.  
* Released 6 vassals from the Mamluks from Syria, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and Anatolia. It will start with a 5 year truce with Anatolian vassals.  
  * *Note: These don’t necessarily decrease power by a massive margin, but serve to make the tag consider more regional consolidation before other options.*  
* Severe opinion penalties applied to Mamlukes with countries in the Anatolian region to reduce the likelihood of military access being granted.  
* The Mamluks start with a new government reform designed to foster an early-game balance.   
  * **Bahri Mamluk Sultanate:** \-33% Peasant Levy Size, \+0.10 Legitimacy, \+0.10 Free Subjects. Removable when the **Professional Armies** institution is **embraced**.
