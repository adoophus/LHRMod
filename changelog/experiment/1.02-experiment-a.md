## LHR EU5 1.02 Experiment A

### Goal

Currently a singular auxillary supplies a 35 frontage army for 18 days.\
A single supply convoy unit should carry enough food to supply a `35` frontage light infantry army for `1.5 months`.\
A base supply hub should supply double this amount. (3 months)

### Source Values Used
- Light infantry category food consumption per strength: `0.33`
- Variables:
  - Frontage target: `35 units in army`
  - Daily food = `(food usage * frontage target)`
  - Monthly food = `(daily * 30)`
- Supply goal: `1.5 months` (45 days)

### Proposed Supply Carts Scaling (Age 1-5)
